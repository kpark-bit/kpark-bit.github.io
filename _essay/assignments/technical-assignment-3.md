I created a public Zotero group library focusing on Korean environmental history. I began by uploading about twenty items from my own collection, including scholarship in both Korean and English. Because I imported the files as they were, and because I had previously divided some books into individual chapters, many entries still have incomplete bibliographic information. Zotero also tended to prioritize English titles when Korean-language articles provided titles in both languages. As a result, the library’s list of entries does not adequately reflect the Korean-language scholarship it contains. During winter break, I will organize the metadata and specify each item’s original title by language.


My criteria for future additions are related with what I hope this collection can achieve. Graduate students working on Korean environmental history are few and geographically scattered. To my knowledge, only one other student began a PhD in this field in the US the same year I did, and that student is in California. I therefore hope to use this library as a shared resource for a reading group that brings graduate students in Korea and the US together over Zoom and Zotero. For now, the reading list contains only historical works. Yet environmental history is greatly interdisciplinary, and as membership grows, the library could expand to include works in adjacent fields such as sociology and geography.
